========================================
  Welcome to Cyber-Bot FTP Pentest Lab
========================================

This is an intentionally vulnerable FTP service for isolated training.

Challenge:
1. Find the anonymous-access flag fragment.
2. Discover the weak password for the ftpuser account.
3. Retrieve and crack the password-protected backup archive.
4. Combine the three flag fragments.

Only test this lab on a machine/network you own or are explicitly authorized to test.
